Reading Guide Reference

< 80 µm (Thin Paint)

Excessive machine polishing or poor factory coating. Susceptible to damage.

80 – 140 µm (Factory Standard)

Original OEM specification. Uniform, high-quality protective layer.

> 180 µm (Repaint / Body Filler)

Indicates panel repair, filler application, and subsequent repaint from dealer/yard damage.

Factory Coating

Scan coordinates indicate uniform factory finish. No signs of panel removal or cosmetic respraying.
