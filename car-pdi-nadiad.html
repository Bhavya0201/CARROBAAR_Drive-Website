What We Check in 300+ Inspection Points

Paint Thickness Micron Scan

Digital magnetic coating gauge checks all metal panels. We spot factory rework, repainted bumpers, blend coats, and transport damage instantly.

OBD-II Diagnostic Computer Scan

We scan the vehicle ECU for engine error codes, ABS sensor faults, airbag trigger history, electrical misfires, and hidden odometer tampering.

Underbody & Suspension Audit

Inspection of suspension arms, chassis welds, exhaust plumbing, fluid leaks, missing underbody shield bolts, and transit scraping.
