Conflict of Interest

Why Independent Inspections Matter

Car buying involves three different stakeholders. Understanding their incentives helps you make the right choice.

The Dealership's Goal

To register and hand over the car as fast as possible to secure sales commissions. They often overlook storage yard transit damages, minor scratches, battery issues, or alignment defects.

The Buyer's Goal

To get a brand-new, perfect car with zero defects and high resale value. Buyers do not have specialized tools (like paint meters and OBD2 scanners) to catch hidden showroom defects.

Our Goal

We are 100% unbiased. We do not sell cars or take dealer commissions. Our only incentive is to protect you, catch defects before registration, and save you from expensive post-delivery repairs.

Critical Inspection Audit

Outdoor Stockyard Rain & Flood Water Logging Risk

Cars parked in open dealer stockyards during monsoon rains frequently suffer from water logging, silt deposits inside chassis rails, seat runner rust, and submerged OBD ECU connectors. Dealerships quickly detail the exterior paint before delivery, masking severe hidden water damage. Our 300+ point audit checks behind wheel arch liners, carpet underlays, fuse box pins, and underbody crevices to ensure you never take delivery of a flood-damaged vehicle.

1. Odometer Tampering

Over 35% of pre-owned cars in India undergo meter rollback. We run deep OBD-II ECU diagnostic scans to compare stored engine operational hours vs cluster reading, catching hidden meter reversal traps.

2. Chassis Frame & Weld Damage

Unreported accident repairs often mask repainted crumple zone welds and pulled chassis rails. We inspect underbody member alignment to protect you from buying unsafe, compromised vehicles.

3. Engine Sludge & Leaks

Skipped engine oil changes leave heavy carbon sludge under the valve cover and head gasket leaks. We perform compression and oil sludging checks to prevent post-purchase engine overhauls.
