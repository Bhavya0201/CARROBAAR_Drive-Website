Mahindra & MG Motor VIN FAQs

In Mahindra 17-digit VINs (starting with MA1), the 10th character is the year code (P=2023, R=2024, S=2025, T=2026) and the 12th character indicates the month of manufacture.

Mahindra ladder-frame vehicles have the 17-digit VIN stamped directly into the right-hand chassis frame rail visible through the front right wheel well, as well as on the driver door sill plate.

MG Motor uses standard global VIN formatting where the 10th character identifies the year. The exact production date is also printed on the black vehicle compliance plate on the passenger side B-pillar.
