Where to Locate Your Hyundai & Kia Chassis Number

1) Stamped directly into the chassis floor pan underneath the driver's seat carpet flap; 2) Black barcode sticker on the front passenger B-pillar; 3) Front windscreen driver side lower corner; 4) Form 22 and vehicle tax invoice.
