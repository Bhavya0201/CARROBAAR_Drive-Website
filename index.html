Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our car inspection services serving in Gujarat.

A Pre-Delivery Inspection is a thorough inspection of a brand new car before you take delivery from the dealer. Showroom cars are often transported long distances, stored in open yards, or used for test drives. During this, they can suffer scratches, structural damage, repaints, or engine issues. We inspect the vehicle in the showroom *before* registration to ensure you get a completely flawless vehicle.
Dealerships perform a basic checklist mainly focusing on washing, cleaning, fluids check, and obvious transit damage. Their main incentive is to get the vehicle registered and delivered to secure their commission. CARROBAAR_Drive is 100% independent. We perform a highly technical 300+ point audit—using tools like paint thickness meters and OBD2 diagnostic scanners—to inspect panel alignment, transit damage repaints, computer logs, and hidden electrical faults that dealers might skip.
The most common issues we catch include transit resprays (damage caused during trucking/yard storage that dealers paint over), misaligned panel gaps, failing cabin electronics (non-glowing switches, faulty touchscreens), missing engine undercover push-lock buttons, low fluid levels, and battery health degradation due to cars sitting in holding yards for months. Catching these before registration allows you to get them replaced for free.
We use precision digital coating thickness gauges (paint depth meters) on all body panels. Factory paint thickness usually ranges between 90 to 150 microns. Consistent readings over 200 microns indicate a panel respray, whereas readings above 350 to 800+ microns confirm the presence of body filler (bondo) from a major repair. We also visually inspect structural chassis welds, suspension aprons, and engine bay seals for signs of welding or collision repair.
Yes. While odometer fraud can sometimes be hidden, advanced diagnostic scanners can read data directly from independent modules rather than just the instrument cluster. We access live data from the Transmission Control Module (TCM), ABS module, and Body Control Module (BCM) to cross-check stored mileage parameters. If these modules show higher mileage than the odometer display on the dashboard, it is a clear sign of odometer rollback.
We check for engine oil sludge by inspecting the oil dipstick and oil filler cap for a milky, frothy "chocolate milkshake" emulsion—which indicates coolant leaking into the engine oil via a blown head gasket. Additionally, we check the coolant reservoir for exhaust gas bubbles while running, check tailpipe exhaust smoke (blue indicates burning oil, thick white indicates coolant leak), and check engine blow-by under load during a cold start.

A comprehensive Used Car or New Car PDI inspection takes about 1.5 to 2.5 hours depending on vehicle type. We evaluate the mechanicals, structure, electrical components, and perform test drives.
