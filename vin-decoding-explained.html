Step 1

What is a VIN and Where is it Located?

A Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) is a unique 17-character alphanumeric string assigned to every motor vehicle when manufactured. It serves as your car's finger-print, containing coded factory information about the manufacturer, model line, engine code, assembly plant, and exact production month and year.

📍 Where to locate your VIN on your car:
  • Stamped on the engine bay firewall (bulkhead).
  • On a metal plaque at the bottom corner of the front windshield.
  • On the driver or passenger side B-pillar door frame.
  • Under the driver seat frame beneath a flap.
  • Printed on your RTO Registration Certificate (RC) & Tax Invoice.

Step 2

The 3 Core Sections of a VIN

Every 17-digit VIN is structured into three standardized segments:

CHARACTERS 1 – 3

WMI (World Manufacturer Identifier)

Identifies country of origin and car brand (e.g. MA3 for Maruti, MAT for Tata, MAL for Hyundai).

CHARACTERS 4 – 9

VDS (Vehicle Descriptor Section)

Encodes body style, engine variant, transmission type, and security check digit (position 9).

CHARACTERS 10 – 17

VIS (Vehicle Identifier Section)

Contains Model Year, Assembly Plant Code, Month Code, and unique factory serial number.

Why VIN Manufacturing Month Audit Prevents Monsoon Flood Damage

Cars manufactured or stored in open dealer yards during peak monsoon months (June – September) are at severe risk of outdoor stockyard water logging. Rainwater collects inside subframe crevices, causing hidden seat runner rust, carpet mildew, and corroded ECU pins. Matching the VIN production date against stockyard arrival logs helps our inspectors flag water-logged batches before you take delivery.
