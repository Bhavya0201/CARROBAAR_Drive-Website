Inspection Summary & Verdict

Following a comprehensive 180+ point Pre-Delivery Inspection of this Maruti Suzuki WagonR, the vehicle is overall in excellent mechanical and electrical condition, receiving a health score of 94/100. However, three minor defects were identified across different sections: Section 2 (Exterior & Paint) has a paint depth anomaly of 152 µm on the left rear door indicating a minor dealer touch-up repaint; Section 4 (Cabin Interior) has a small grease smudge on the front passenger seat side bolster; and Section 10 (Accessories & Handover) has a loose plastic fastener clip on the right rear mudguard. All other inspected areas, including the engine bay, chassis structure, OBD2 electronics, and the factory-fitted CNG fuel lines, passed in flawless factory-fresh condition with zero faults detected.
